Per-site settings

A setting for a site also covers the addresses under it, so example.com covers www.example.com too.

JavaScript can follow the main switch on the settings page ({{scriptsGlobal}}), or be allowed or blocked for one site. Blocked cookies are never stored or sent for that site. Plain HTTP only matters while HTTPS-only is on ({{httpsOnly}}): an allowed site may load without encryption, every other site has to use HTTPS.
